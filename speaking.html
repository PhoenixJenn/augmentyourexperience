On Stage and On Screen

I've been speaking at conferences, meetups, and company events since 2017 — on topics ranging from surviving a technical interview to what it actually feels like to build in virtual reality during a pandemic. Most of my talks sit at the intersection of emerging technology and the humans trying to use it.

I'm particularly energized by audiences who are curious but skeptical — people who want to understand what spatial computing, AI, or XR actually means for their work, without the hype. If that sounds like your event, let's talk.
